## What is SBTI?

SBTI (Survival & Bullshit Type Indicator) is a satirical workplace personality test app. Answer 25 office-life scenarios and get your personalized "worker archetype" diagnostic report. Available in English and Chinese.

## Features

- 🎮 25 witty quiz questions
- 🧬 16 unique personality types
- 📊 Radar chart across 4 workplace dimensions
- 🖼️ Generate & share diagnostic poster
- 🌐 English & Chinese support
